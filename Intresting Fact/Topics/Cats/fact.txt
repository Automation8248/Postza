Cats sleep for about twelve to sixteen hours every single day.
A cat's nose print is unique, just like a human fingerprint.
A group of cats is called a clowder.
Cats have a third eyelid that helps protect and moisten their eyes.
Each cat ear has about thirty-two muscles, so it can rotate to catch tiny sounds.
Cats can jump up to five or six times their own body length.
Adult cats have around two hundred and thirty bones, while humans have only two hundred and six.
Cats cannot taste sweetness, because they lack the receptor for it.
Adult cats rarely meow at each other. They mostly meow to talk to humans.
A cat's whiskers help it sense nearby objects and judge whether it can fit through a gap.
Cats have a field of view of about two hundred degrees.
Cats can see in light that is about six times dimmer than what humans need.
Cats spend up to a third of their waking hours grooming themselves.
A cat purrs at a frequency of about twenty-five to one hundred and fifty hertz.
The oldest known pet cat remains were found in Cyprus and are about nine thousand five hundred years old.
