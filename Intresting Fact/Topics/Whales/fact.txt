The blue whale is the largest animal known to have ever lived on Earth.
The tongue of a blue whale can weigh as much as an adult elephant.
Humpback whales sing long, complex songs that can travel for many miles underwater.
Sperm whales have the largest brain of any animal on the planet.
Bowhead whales can live for more than two hundred years.
Whales are mammals, so they breathe air and feed their calves with milk.
Cuvier's beaked whale holds the record for the deepest dive of any mammal, at nearly three thousand meters.
Blue whale calls can reach about one hundred eighty-eight decibels, among the loudest sounds made by any animal.
The long tusk of a narwhal is actually a tooth that grows through its lip.
Killer whales, also called orcas, are actually the largest members of the dolphin family.
Many whales sleep with only half of their brain at a time, so they can keep breathing.
Gray whales migrate more than ten thousand miles every year, one of the longest journeys of any mammal.
Whale poop helps fertilize the ocean and feeds tiny plankton.
Humpback whales sometimes blow a spiral net of bubbles to trap fish before they feed.
