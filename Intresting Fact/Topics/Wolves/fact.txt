Wolves are the largest members of the dog family.
In open land, a wolf's howl can be heard from about six miles away.
A wolf pack is usually a family, made of parents and their young.
Wolves can run at about thirty-five miles per hour for short distances.
An adult wolf has forty-two teeth.
Wolf pups are born deaf and blind, and they open their eyes after about two weeks.
A wolf can travel more than twenty miles in a single day.
Every wolf has its own unique howl.
A hungry wolf can eat up to twenty pounds of meat in one meal.
Wolves have a sense of smell far better than humans.
Wolves were brought back to Yellowstone National Park in nineteen ninety-five, and they changed the whole ecosystem.
All pet dogs descended from ancient wolves.
