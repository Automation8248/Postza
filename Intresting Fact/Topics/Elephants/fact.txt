The African elephant is the largest land animal on Earth.
An elephant's trunk has tens of thousands of muscles, so it can lift a log or pick up a single peanut.
Elephants are pregnant for about twenty-two months, the longest pregnancy of any land mammal.
Elephants can recognize themselves in a mirror, which shows they are self-aware.
Elephants cannot jump, but they are surprisingly good swimmers.
Elephants can feel low rumbles from far away through the ground with their feet.
An elephant's tusks are actually very long front teeth.
Wild elephants sleep only about two to four hours a day.
Elephants spend most of the day eating and can munch hundreds of pounds of plants every day.
Baby elephants suck on their trunks, just like human babies suck on their thumbs.
Elephants appear to show grief, and they sometimes gather around the bones of their dead relatives.
An elephant has the largest brain of any land animal.
Elephants flap their huge ears to cool down their blood in the hot sun.
